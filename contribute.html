Contribute to DataForSolution

Share practical knowledge that helps others learn and build

DataForSolution welcomes thoughtful, original contributions that support learning, responsible technology, healthcare intelligence, analytics, medical imaging, data science, and software engineering.

What you can contribute

Projects

A reproducible project built around a clearly defined real-world problem.

Tools

A useful browser tool, notebook, template, calculator, or workflow resource.

Case Studies

A structured explanation of a problem, method, result, limitation, and lesson learned.

Learning Resources

Tutorials, reference guides, exercises, or technical explanations that help others progress.

Healthcare & Imaging Insight

Educational material grounded in clinical workflow, medical imaging, informatics, or healthcare analytics.

Responsible AI

Work that improves transparency, safety, privacy, evaluation, fairness, or human oversight.

Contribution standard

Useful, honest, and grounded

Submissions should be original, clearly explained, respectful of privacy and intellectual property, and honest about assumptions and limitations. Healthcare-related contributions must be educational and must not include patient-identifying information.

Before submitting
  • Explain the problem and intended audience
  • Provide a working link or repository when applicable
  • State data sources and licensing
  • Describe limitations and validation
  • Remove confidential or personally identifiable information
