Contribution types

What the platform can review

Contributions do not need to be large. They do need to be useful, original, clearly sourced, and honest about limitations.

Projects & code

Reproducible projects, notebooks, examples, or engineering work built around a clearly described problem.

Tools & templates

Calculators, browser tools, reference templates, workflow aids, or reusable technical resources.

Articles & tutorials

Original explanations, practical guides, technical walkthroughs, or lessons that help users make informed decisions.

Case studies

Structured accounts of a problem, method, evidence, result, limitation, and lesson learned.

Healthcare & imaging insight

Educational material grounded in clinical workflow, medical imaging, informatics, analytics, or professional practice.

Resource recommendations

High-quality datasets, documentation, media, standards, or learning sources that improve the curated directories.

Shared standard

Useful, honest, and safe

Whether the goal is collaboration or contribution, the same baseline applies: define the problem, identify who is affected, explain the evidence, respect licensing and privacy, and be clear about what has not been validated.

Before submitting
  • Explain the problem, audience, or intended use.
  • Provide a working repository or source link when applicable.
  • State data sources, licensing, and attribution requirements.
  • Describe validation, assumptions, and important limitations.
  • Do not include patient-identifying, confidential, or proprietary information.
  • Do not present educational healthcare work as clinical advice or validated clinical performance.
