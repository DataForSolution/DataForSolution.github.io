Working principle

Start with the problem, not the technology

A useful collaboration should identify the people affected, the current workflow, available data, constraints, risks, and the decision that needs improvement before selecting a technical approach.

Good starting information
  • The problem or workflow you want to improve
  • Who uses the current process
  • What data or systems are involved
  • The expected outcome or decision
  • Any privacy, safety, or operational constraints
